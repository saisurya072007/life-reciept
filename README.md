# 🎟️ Your Life, In Receipts

### Turning disconnected digital-life records into meaningful interactive stories.

---

## 🚀 Overview

**Your Life, In Receipts** is a frontend-only digital storytelling platform that transforms scattered digital-life records into meaningful patterns, relationships, and interactive stories.

Digital activities such as searches, music, movies, purchases, places, photos, messages, events, and notes may look like unrelated records when viewed individually.

This project looks at them together.

Instead of simply displaying a timeline or dashboard, the system analyzes the uploaded records, discovers relationships between them, identifies recurring topics and patterns, and turns connected records into interactive stories.
